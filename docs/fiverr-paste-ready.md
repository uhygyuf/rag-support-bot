## 6. FAQ (6 entries, each ≤400 chars)

**Q1: What exactly do I get?**
```
A working AI chat assistant on your website, trained on your documents, plus the complete n8n workflow file, setup guide and an acceptance checklist. You own everything after delivery.
```
**Q2: Do I pay a monthly fee to you?**
```
No. You pay once. The assistant runs in your n8n workspace with your own AI key and database, so there is no subscription to me and no lock-in.
```
**Q3: What do you need from me to start?**
```
Your documents (FAQ, policies, product info or PDFs), your website address and an AI API key (DeepSeek or OpenAI). If you do not have a key yet, I send a 2-minute guide.
```
**Q4: What happens when it does not know the answer?**
```
It never invents one. It says it cannot answer, saves the question as a ticket and tells the customer a human will follow up - so you keep the lead instead of letting it disappear.
```
**Q5: Can it be connected to WhatsApp, Slack or my CRM?**
```
Website chat and ticket storage are in every package. Telegram and email answer channels, plus CRM/webhook integration, are in Premium - tell me what you use and I will confirm before you order.
```
**Q6: What if an answer is wrong?**
```
Send me the question and the correct answer. Rules are tuned within your revision scope and the knowledge base updated. Unsure questions are never answered automatically.
```

## 9. Rules that protect the account (do not break these)

1. All communication inside Fiverr. Never move a buyer to email/WhatsApp, never leave contact details.
2. Only promise what is verified — the ✅ rows in `docs/gig-copy-n8n-chatbot.md`.
3. Never promise "zero errors" or a specific accuracy number; sell "reversible, unsure questions are
   never answered, improves as you use it".
4. Say "set up / I'll set up", not "I built" for tools you configure.
5. Delivering AI-generated work is allowed; hiding it is not.
