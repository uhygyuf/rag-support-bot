# Harbor Coffee Roasters — Company and policies

## About us
Family-run roastery in Portland, Maine, founded 2019. Four people: two roasters, one packer, one support.
We buy green coffee through two importers and pay above the Fair Trade minimum for every lot.
Roastery tours on Fridays at 3pm, free, no booking needed.

## Privacy (short version)
- We store your name, delivery address and order history to fulfil orders.
- We never sell customer data. Card details are handled by our payment provider, not stored by us.
- You can ask us to delete your account data at any time by emailing privacy@harborcoffee.example.

## Environmental policy
- Bags are recyclable kraft paper with a compostable valve; we use no plastic tape.
- Shipping boxes are 90% recycled. We offset shipping emissions through a yearly contribution.

## Terms (short version)
- Coffee is a food product: replacements are offered for quality problems, no cash refunds on opened bags.
- Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled.
- Prices can change with the green coffee market; existing subscriptions keep their price for 6 months.

## Things we do NOT do
- We do not offer franchise opportunities.
- We do not sell green (unroasted) coffee below 5 kg.
- We do not take orders over the phone; the website or email only.
