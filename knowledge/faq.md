# Harbor Coffee Roasters — Support FAQ

## Orders and shipping
- We roast and ship on **Tuesdays and Fridays**. Orders placed before 9am on a roast day ship the same day.
- **US delivery** takes 2–4 business days after shipping (USPS Priority).
- **International shipping**: available to the EU, UK, Canada and Australia — 7–14 business days. Customs duties are paid by the customer.
- Shipping is free on US orders over **$45**. Below that it is a flat $6.50.
- Tracking numbers are emailed as soon as the label is created. If you don't see it, check spam, then email support@harborcoffee.example.

## Subscriptions
- Subscriptions ship every **2 or 4 weeks**; you choose the cadence at checkout.
- Pause or skip a delivery from your account up to **24 hours before the roast day**. After that the box is already roasted and packed.
- Subscribers get 10% off every bag and free US shipping regardless of order size.
- Cancel anytime — no minimum term, no fee. Cancelling before the next roast day stops that shipment.

## Products and freshness
- Everything is roasted to order; bags carry a **roast date**, not a best-before date.
- **Whole bean or ground**: we grind free of charge — choose espresso, filter, French press or moka at checkout.
- Best flavour within **6 weeks of the roast date**. Freeze the bag for longer storage; do not refrigerate.
- Decaf is Swiss Water Process. We also offer one **single-origin** rotating monthly and two blends (Harbor House, Night Watch).

## Returns and problems
- If a bag arrives damaged, or the coffee tastes off, contact us within **30 days** and we replace it free — no need to return the bag.
- Wrong grind or wrong item: we ship the correct bag the same day and you keep the first one.
- We do not accept returns of opened coffee for hygiene reasons, but replacements cover any quality problem.

## Wholesale and business
- Wholesale pricing starts at **5 kg per month** (mixed or single origin). Lead time is 7 business days.
- We offer private-label roasting for cafés and offices: your label, our roast, minimum 10 kg per month.
- For wholesale, private label, press or partnership enquiries, a human needs to handle it: email wholesale@harborcoffee.example.

## Payment and misc
- We accept Visa, Mastercard, Amex, PayPal and Apple Pay. We do not accept cash on delivery.
- We are a small team of four; support is answered **Monday to Friday, 9am–5pm US Eastern**. Outside those hours a human replies the next business day.
